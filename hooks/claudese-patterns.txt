# Colon-pivot bullets: "- Label: elaboration" prose instead of a sentence.
# Short capitalized label, colon, lowercase prose continuation. Key-value
# data lines usually have capitalized or backticked values, so they escape.
# (?-i: ...) turns off the file-wide ignore-case, which this rule needs: the
# capital is the whole signal, and without it "- custom: combine ..." matches.
(?-i:^\s*[-*•] +[A-Z][a-z][^:\n]{2,30}: [a-z])
